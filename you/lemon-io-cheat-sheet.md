# Lemon.io — Vetting Interview — Cheat Sheet
Vetted freelance developer marketplace (top ~1.2% accepted) · Wed 10:00am-12:15pm · Stage: technical interview (only ~3% of screened applicants reach this stage)

## What this actually is
Not a single-employer interview — passing gets you **into their developer network**, then matched to client startups afterward (contract/freelance work). The bar is proving senior-level competence + communication, not "why this company." Format per their own email: friendly conversation about professional experience → theoretical questions → practical task(s). 2h15m block suggests real depth, likely including live coding.

## Your one-liner (marketplace framing, not company framing)
"15 years, mostly React/TypeScript, specializing in design systems and frontend tooling — and I'm used to moving across very different codebases and teams: five different companies plus running my own open-source tooling venture. That range is exactly what project-based client work needs."

## Why you're a strong fit for THIS format specifically
- You're already doing short contract engagements right now (Handshake, micro1, Mercor — AI model evaluation, all 1-2 month contracts) — genuine, current proof you operate well in project-based/contract work, not just full-time roles.
- ForgeKit is an independent venture — you're comfortable self-directed, without a manager structure telling you what to do.
- Career spans 5 different companies/codebases (Freebird, PHC, Pineapple, Redesign Health, FaceCake) — fast ramp-up on unfamiliar codebases is a real, provable skill, not a claim.

## What they'll likely probe
- **Theoretical questions** — core JS/TS (closures, event loop, hoisting, `this`, prototypal inheritance, React rendering/reconciliation, hooks rules, performance — memoization, virtualization). Know the *why*, not just the definition.
- **Practical task(s)** — expect something similar in shape to the ElevenLabs CoderPad practice you already ran (fix a bug, finish a component, maybe a small logic/algorithm problem). Warm up on `elevenlabs-fe-practice/` and `ramp-fe-practice/` again the morning of.
- **Communication / English fluency** — explicitly part of what they screen for (per their vetting docs) since you'll work directly with founders/clients with no PM buffer. Speak in complete, clear explanations; narrate your reasoning out loud during any live coding.
- **"Tell me about your experience"** — this is where the range (contract eval work, 5 companies, ForgeKit) does the work; don't undersell the contract gigs as a gap, frame them as current, active, relevant proof.

## Questions to ask them
- "What does matching actually look like once someone's in the network — do I see and choose opportunities, or are they assigned?"
- "What's the typical engagement length and rate range for someone at my level?"
- "How much of the work is React/TypeScript specifically vs. broader stacks?"

- **Don't apologize for the contract-work stretch** — this interview is literally testing for comfort with exactly that mode of working. Lean in.
- **Rate expectations** — if asked, have a number ready beforehand; don't improvise one live. (Not filled in here — decide this ahead of Wednesday.)
- **No company-specific "why us" prep needed** — don't waste prep time on Lemon.io-the-company; spend it on JS/TS fundamentals + a timed practice rep.
- Distinct from RainFocus later that day (5pm) — this one is about proving general senior-level skill for a marketplace, not fit for one specific product/team.
