## The honest gap: Svelte / Web Components
Your production depth is React (15 years) — Svelte and Web Components aren't on your resume. Don't bluff fluency. Two honest, confident moves:
- Ask directly and early: "Is the stack split React/Svelte/Web Components across different parts of the platform, or is one primary and the others legacy/edge cases?" — their answer tells you how much this actually matters.
- Frame it straight: "My depth is React — 15 years, including component-library and architecture work at the level this role needs. I haven't shipped production Svelte or Web Components, but the underlying architectural judgment (component API design, state boundaries, performance) transfers directly, and I ramp on new frameworks fast — that's basically what founding ForgeKit forced me to do across React, React Native, and multiple UI kits."
