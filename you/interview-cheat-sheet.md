# Interview Cheat Sheet (general)
## 30-sec opener
"Senior frontend engineer, ~15 years, mostly React/TypeScript. Niche is the systems layer — design systems, component libraries, tooling that makes teams ship faster. Founded ForgeKit (Figma→React, ~5,700 installs). IC to Engineering Director at Redesign. Most excited where AI tooling meets frontend." Say it slow, then stop.
## North star
"I build the systems that make product teams faster."
## Three stories
A. ForgeKit — initiative + AI fluency (solo, MCP, 5,700 installs).
B. Redesign design system — leadership + impact (50+ components, 30% cut, onboarding method).
C. FaceCake/NARS — range + revenue (~$400K/mo; Emscripten browser CV).
## Tough Qs
"What have you been up to?" → ForgeKit (gap = intentional entrepreneurship).
"Why leave Redesign?" → "Took the design system as far as it needed; wanted to build my own."
"Weaker area?" → "Deep backend — solid on Node/API, lean on specialists for heavy data infra; close gaps fast."

## Master answer for ANY domain/tech gap (voice AI, game dev, growth eng, Svelte, healthcare, whatever)
Don't bluff depth you don't have. Own it plainly, then pivot to the actual pattern of your career:
> "I learn as I go — it's quite literally how I've kept running at a startup. [Domain] specifically isn't my deep background, but ramping fast on unfamiliar territory is the actual job I've done across five different 0-to-1 companies and my own venture. I'd rather be straight about where I'm starting from than pretend otherwise."
Use this everywhere a specific gap comes up (voice AI depth for ElevenLabs/Weekend, Svelte for RainFocus, formal A/B/growth tooling for Nivoda, healthcare domain for Mochi) instead of a bespoke excuse each time — it's one honest, consistent story, and it's true.
## Reset moves
"Let me think for a sec." Breathe. Bridge to a story. Shorten, don't spiral.
