# Perch — brand kit (v0)

**Name:** Perch — a friendly voice assistant that "perches" on any website.
**Tagline:** *Give your site a voice.*
**One-liner:** Drop-in voice AI for any website — one script tag, powered by ElevenLabs.

## Colors
- Iris (primary):   #6D5EF6
- Aqua (accent):    #22C1C3
- Ink (text):       #151726
- Slate (muted):    #667085
- Cloud (bg):       #F6F7F9
- White:            #FFFFFF
- Gradient: Iris → Aqua (135deg)

## Type
System stack: `Segoe UI, Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif` (swap for Inter later).
Headings 700, body 400.

## Voice & tone
Warm, plain, dev-friendly. Confident, not salesy. Short sentences.
