Live in one line

Your visitor taps the orb and talks. Your ElevenLabs key never leaves your server.

1
Widget

The Perch orb calls your session endpoint when a visitor starts talking.

2
Your server

Mints a short-lived signed URL from ElevenLabs. The API key stays server-side.

3
ElevenLabs

Runs the live WebRTC voice session — speech in, agent voice out.

🔒 Signed URLs expire in ~15 min and are minted per session. Your key is never in the browser.
